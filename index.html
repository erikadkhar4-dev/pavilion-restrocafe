The Place

Comfort food, cooked slow enough to matter.

The Pavilion Restrocafe sits just off the Aapata–Dandfata road near Tulasi Hotel, Rasayani — a covered, plant-draped courtyard that keeps its regulars coming back for the ambience as much as the plates.

The kitchen leans into fresh dough pizzas, gourmet burgers and loaded fries, with a drinks list built around mojitos and a hot chocolate the town has opinions about. Expect a relaxed pace, low music and staff who remember your usual order.

Good for birthdays, slow lunches, and evenings that start with garlic bread and don't really end.
